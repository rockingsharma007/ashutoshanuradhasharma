## The one-sentence answer

Air molecules scatter short (blue) wavelengths of sunlight far more strongly
than long (red) ones. That's **Rayleigh scattering**, and it scales with the
inverse fourth power of wavelength.

## Then why are sunsets red?

At sunset, light travels through much more atmosphere. The blue has already
been scattered away before it reaches you, leaving the reds and oranges.

> Same physics, opposite result — it just depends on how much air the light
> has to cross.
