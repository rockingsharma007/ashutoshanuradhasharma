## Room to breathe

Good design isn't about filling space — it's about deciding what to leave
out. Whitespace directs attention, sets rhythm, and signals confidence.

## A small exercise

Take any layout you admire and cover the content. Look only at the gaps.
You'll often find the empty regions are more carefully proportioned than the
filled ones.

Swap this out for your own writing on music, literature, or design.
