## The core idea

Compounding is earning returns on your returns. Small, steady growth becomes
dramatic over long horizons — the curve bends upward slowly, then all at once.

## Why time beats timing

An investor who starts ten years earlier, but contributes less overall, often
ends up ahead of a later, larger investor. The extra decade of compounding
does the heavy lifting.
